# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers and product managers who need to manage feature flags.

## Product Purpose

flaggable.dev is a feature flagging platform that enables developers and product managers to manage feature flags. Success means managing flags feels modern, stable, and user-friendly.

## Positioning

The product is positioned as a modern, stable, and user-friendly feature flagging platform for both developers and product managers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Feature flag management is the confirmed core capability.
- The product must serve both technical and product audiences.
- Specific flag workflows, integrations, permissions, environments, rollout controls, and deployment constraints remain undecided.

## Product Principles

- Make feature flag management easy to understand.
- Support developers and product managers in the same product.
- Favor dependable behavior and clear feedback.
- Keep future workflows approachable and user-friendly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility requirements have been established yet.
